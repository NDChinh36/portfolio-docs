BA Design Notes & Annotations
1
Centralized exception queue: All operational issues flow into a single structured queue, replacing fragmented Zalo group messages and Excel sheets that caused resolution delays, missed items, and zero audit trail.
2
SLA countdown prioritization: Displaying remaining time rather than creation time enables triage by urgency — critical and near-breach exceptions surface at the top, reducing cognitive overhead for ops staff.
3
Resolution note + audit trail: Every resolved exception must include a resolution note, creating an auditable history used for root-cause analysis, SLA reporting, and carrier dispute evidence.
4
Suggested action field: System-generated action guidance reduces analysis time per exception — the assignee can act immediately without consulting docs or escalating for guidance on routine exception types.
