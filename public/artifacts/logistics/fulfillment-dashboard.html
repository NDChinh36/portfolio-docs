BA Design Notes & Annotations
1
Separate status tracking: OMS order status, WMS fulfillment status, and carrier shipment status are tracked as independent state machines — this is a core design decision enabling each system to evolve without coupling.
2
SLA breach column: Surfaces real-time SLA remaining per order so operations managers can detect bottlenecks and prioritize intervention before customer impact occurs.
3
Exception queue: Centralizes all operational issues in a structured queue, replacing ad-hoc Zalo group messages and manual Excel tracking that caused resolution delays and no audit trail.
4
Carrier pickup queue: Aggregates pending handover volumes by carrier with cut-off time visibility, directly supporting daily dispatch planning and preventing missed pickups.
