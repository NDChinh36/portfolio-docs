BA Design Notes
1 Appointment status, check-in status and HIS sync status are THREE separate fields — each has its own lifecycle and system owner. They must never be collapsed into a single "status" column.
2 Receptionist can retry HIS sync from the dashboard without navigating to a separate screen — reducing task-switching during peak hours.
3 Queue number is generated ONLY after successful HIS sync — never before. This prevents patients from holding a queue number that cannot be processed by the doctor's system.
4 HIS Sync Failed appears as orange badge, prompting immediate action before the patient waits too long. Row is highlighted to draw attention without a disruptive pop-up.
5 Filters help manage 128 daily appointments during peak hours — allowing receptionist to quickly isolate failed syncs, pending confirmations, or specific doctors' queues.
