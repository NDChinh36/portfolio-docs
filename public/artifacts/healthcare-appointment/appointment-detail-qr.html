BA Design Notes
1
QR Valid Time Window
QR is only active from 60 minutes before appointment to 30 minutes after. Outside this window, scan is rejected with a clear error. Prevents patients from arriving too early or scanning stale codes.
BR-CHECKIN-01
2
Cancel/Reschedule Disabled After Cut-off
After cut-off time (2 hours before appointment), these actions are disabled on the patient side. Only a receptionist with override permission can cancel or reschedule. Buttons remain visible to avoid confusion but are non-interactive with tooltip explanation.
BR-CANCEL-01 / BR-CANCEL-02
3
Appointment Status vs Check-in Status
"CONFIRMED" is the appointment lifecycle state. Check-in is a separate event triggered by QR scan or manual action. These must never be merged — a patient can be Confirmed but Not Checked-in.
Key design decision
4
QR is One-Time Use Only
QR token is invalidated immediately after a successful scan. A second scan of the same code returns an "already used" error. This prevents duplicate check-ins and queue number assignments.
BR-CHECKIN-05
5
HIS Registration Trigger
Successful QR scan triggers the HIS registration API call automatically — NOT the check-in button click by staff. This ensures the trigger point is unambiguous and auditable regardless of which staff member processes the queue.
BR-HIS-01
