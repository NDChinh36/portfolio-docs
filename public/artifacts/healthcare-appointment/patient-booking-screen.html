BA Design Notes
1
Slot Hold Timer
Slot is locked for 10 minutes while patient fills the form. Auto-expires and releases slot if not submitted within the window — preventing ghost reservations.
BR-BOOK-02
2
Required Fields Validation
full_name, DOB, identity_number, phone, chief_complaint (min 10 chars) are all required. Submit button is disabled until all validations pass.
BR-BOOK-06
3
Identity Number — Masked Display
CCCD is shown masked (****5678901) for privacy on screen. The full value is encrypted in transit and sent to HIS for patient matching and deduplication.
4
Duplicate Appointment Check
System checks if patient already has a same-day same-specialty appointment before allowing submit. Duplicate detected = block with explanation message.
BR-BOOK-04
5
Booking Cut-off Rule
Cannot book within 2 hours of the appointment time. The slot is hidden from search results once cut-off is reached — not just blocked at submit.
BR-BOOK-05
6
Family Member Flow
If "Người thân" is selected, system requires caregiver authorization link. A secondary contact form appears, and relationship must be declared for audit compliance.
