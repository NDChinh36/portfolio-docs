BA Design Notes
1 HIS Sync Failed is CRITICAL — patient cannot receive a queue number until resolved. SLA = 5 minutes. Exceeding SLA requires mandatory escalation to supervisor with a reason code.
2 Exception queue prevents staff from managing issues through Zalo or phone — all actions, notes, and state transitions are logged in the system for audit and performance review.
3 Resolution requires a reason code — ensures audit trail for compliance. Staff cannot close an exception without selecting a resolution category (e.g., "Retried successfully", "Manual override applied").
4 SLA countdown drives visual priority: red bar = breach imminent, orange = urgent, yellow = normal, blue = low priority. SLA timer starts when exception is first created, not when assigned.
