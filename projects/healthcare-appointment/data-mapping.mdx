## System Data Flow

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    PA["Patient App\nMobile / Web"]
    AS["Appointment Service\nCore Backend"]
    HIS["HIS\nHospital Information System"]
    KR["Kiosk / Reception\nCheck-in Terminal"]
    NS["Notification Service\nSMS / Email / Push"]

    PA -->|"Book / Cancel / Reschedule"| AS
    AS -->|"Register visit + idempotency_key"| HIS
    HIS -->|"Confirm visit_id + queue_number"| AS
    AS -->|"qr_code_token + status"| PA
    KR -->|"QR scan / Manual ID"| AS
    AS -->|"checkin_status + room_number"| KR
    AS -->|"Notification events"| NS
    NS -->|"Delivery status callback"| AS
    HIS -->|"Doctor schedule + slot availability"| AS
```

**Integration Patterns:**
- Patient App ↔ AS: REST API (HTTPS), real-time
- AS ↔ HIS: REST / HL7, synchronous at check-in + polling for visit status
- Kiosk/Reception ↔ AS: REST API, real-time
- AS → NS: Message Queue (Kafka/SQS), event-driven

## Patient Identity Matching Logic

### Match Flow: `identity_number` → `his_patient_code`

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A([Start: Check-in trigger HIS sync]) --> B{Patient có\nhis_patient_code\ncached?}
    B -- Yes --> C[Dùng cached his_patient_code\nSkip HIS lookup]
    B -- No --> D[Gọi HIS Patient Search API\nwith identity_number]
    D --> E{HIS tìm thấy?}
    E -- "Exact Match\n(identity_number khớp 100%)" --> F[Lấy his_patient_code\nCache vào Patient Profile]
    E -- "No match" --> G{Fuzzy Match?\nname + DOB}
    G -- "Match score ≥ 85%" --> H[Flag: FUZZY_MATCH\nYêu cầu Reception xác nhận]
    G -- "No match" --> I[Tạo mới Patient record trong HIS]
    I --> J[HIS trả về his_patient_code mới]
    J --> K[Lưu mapping vào Patient Profile]
    F --> L([Tiếp tục HIS registration])
    H --> M{Reception\nxác nhận?}
    M -- Yes --> F
    M -- No --> I
    K --> L
    C --> L
```

| Case | Điều kiện | Xử lý | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact Match | `identity_number` khớp 100% với HIS record | Dùng `his_patient_code` trực tiếp, cache lại | Thấp |
| Fuzzy Match | Name + DOB match ≥ 85% | Flag `FUZZY_MATCH`, Reception xác nhận thủ công | Trung bình |
| New Patient | Không match bất kỳ record nào trong HIS | HIS tạo mới, trả `his_patient_code` mới | Thấp |
| Multiple Fuzzy Matches | Nhiều record match ≥ threshold | Alert Reception chọn đúng record; không tự động chọn | Cao |
| HIS Unavailable | HIS API timeout/error | Queue ở `HIS_SYNC_PENDING`; retry sau | Trung bình |
